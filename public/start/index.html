From preview to your domain

Launch in your own accounts.

Start with a public sample preview, then customize your copy. Production comes after you approve the real site and contact flow.

Set up a Cloudflare preview

This opens Cloudflare’s account and repository setup. Continuing there can publish a public sample site. It does not connect your domain or turn the demo into a working inquiry form. Review the resulting preview and your account settings before proceeding.

  1. Get a preview working. Use the Cloudflare setup flow or ask Codex to follow the launch guide.
  2. Approve the business site. Replace example facts, choose a real email or existing request link, and test it.
  3. Prepare production. Give Codex the guided launch prompt. Keep preview and production settings separate.
  4. Connect the domain carefully. Preserve existing email and DNS records, authorize the cutover, then verify the website and email.
